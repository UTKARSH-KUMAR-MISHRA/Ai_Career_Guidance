GATE Preparation & Career Snapshot

The roadmap above includes dedicated GATE-EE and Careers/Projects phases — expand them on the canvas for full detail. Quick reference below.

  • Engineering Mathematics — ~13%
  • Electric Circuits & Networks — ~9%
  • Electromagnetic Fields — ~6%
  • Signals & Systems — ~8%
  • Electrical Machines — ~10%
  • Power Systems — ~10%
  • Control Systems — ~8%
  • Power Electronics — ~8%
  • Analog & Digital Electronics — ~8%
  • General Aptitude — ~15%
  • Build strong fundamentals (Circuits, Machines, Power Systems, Control) subject-by-subject.
  • Solve standard textbook problems + previous 15 years' GATE papers subject-wise.
  • Take weekly full-length mock tests once syllabus is 70% complete.
  • Revise formula sheets and short notes in the final 45 days.
  • Analyse every mock test — track weak topics and re-attempt only those.

Where this roadmap leads

Common destinations after completing this roadmap.

  • EV Powertrain / Traction Motor Design Engineer
  • Battery Management System (BMS) Engineer
  • Power Electronics / Traction Inverter Engineer
  • Charging Infrastructure & Grid Integration Engineer
  • Automotive Embedded Software / Functional Safety Engineer
  • R&D Engineer / Graduate Engineer Trainee at a PSU (via GATE)
  • Higher studies — M.Tech / MS in Power Electronics, EV Systems, or Energy Storage
