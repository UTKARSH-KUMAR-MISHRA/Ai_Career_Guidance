📚 Bibliography & References

Curated references spanning device physics, fabrication, materials science, and the modern semiconductor supply chain — from standards bodies to the textbooks fab and process engineers keep on their desks.

IEEE Standards & Papers

  • IEEE Electron Device Society (EDS) publications
  • IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices (T-ED)
  • IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM)
  • IEEE Std 1076/1364/1800 — HDLs (used downstream in chip design)
  • IEEE Xplore Digital Library — ieeexplore.ieee.org (search "CMOS scaling", "EUV lithography", "FinFET")
  • International Electron Devices Meeting (IEDM) proceedings
  • International Roadmap for Devices and Systems (IRDS) — irds.ieee.org

NPTEL Free Courses (IIT Faculty)

  • MOS Devices and Technology — IIT Bombay
  • Fundamentals of Nanoelectronics — IIT Madras
  • Solid State Physics — IIT Kanpur
  • Materials Science and Engineering — IIT Kharagpur
  • All courses free at nptel.ac.in, certification optional

MIT OCW Open Courseware

  • 3.014 Materials Laboratory (semiconductor processing labs)
  • 6.630 Electromagnetics for Electrical Engineers
